---
term: "Material unaccounted for (MUF)"
source: "IAEA Safeguards Glossary 2022"
---
The difference between the book inventory and the measured physical inventory of nuclear material in a material balance area over a period: inputs minus outputs minus what is still there. Never exactly zero in a bulk-handling plant, because every measurement has an uncertainty; the question is whether the MUF is consistent with that uncertainty.
