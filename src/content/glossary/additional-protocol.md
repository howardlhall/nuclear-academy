---
term: "Additional Protocol"
source: "IAEA INFCIRC/540 (Corrected), 1997"
---
A supplement to a state's comprehensive safeguards agreement that gives the IAEA broader information and access, so that it can conclude that a state has no *undeclared* nuclear material or activities — the "broader conclusion." Without it the Agency can conclude only that *declared* material remained in peaceful use.
